# Tea house seat portraits

Created for this project with the built-in ImageGen tool, using the v1.3.3 concept render as a style reference. The table draws the original atlas through a circular mask for each seat.

Prompt: Create a square 2 by 2 sprite atlas of four head-and-shoulders portraits for a Godot mahjong game. Use warm hand-painted Chinese mobile-game anime, a dark tea-house green backdrop and amber lighting. Place centered faces in four equal quadrants. The four characters are a friendly man in a cream shirt and green cardigan, a tousle-haired man in a brown jacket, a woman with a pale pink flower in a bun, and a woman with a red ribbon in her hair and a jade green blouse. Leave out frames, text and UI.
